## Watch & Follow

### YouTube

**Education & Explainers**

- [Psych2Go](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCkJEpR7JmS36tajD34Gp4VA) — Animated psychology explainers on anxiety, trauma, and emotional health with over 12 million subscribers
- [MedCircle](https://www.youtube.com/medcircle) — Interviews with psychiatrists and psychologists breaking down anxiety disorders, CBT, and treatment options
- [HealthyGamerGG](https://www.youtube.com/@HealthyGamerGG) — Harvard-trained psychiatrist Dr. Alok Kanojia ("Dr. K") discusses anxiety, motivation, and mental health, originally aimed at gamers

**Therapist-Led Coaching**

- [Kati Morton](https://www.youtube.com/katimorton) — Licensed marriage and family therapist covering anxiety, panic, and general mental health in accessible videos
- [Therapy in a Nutshell](https://www.youtube.com/c/TherapyinaNutshell) — Emma McAdam, LMFT, condenses CBT and other evidence-based therapy skills into short, practical videos
- [Dr. Tracey Marks](https://www.youtube.com/drtraceymarks) — Board-certified psychiatrist explaining anxiety disorders, medication, and treatment options

### Instagram

- [@theanxietyhealer](https://www.instagram.com/theanxietyhealer/) — Alison Seponara, LPC, shares coping strategies and mindfulness techniques for anxiety and panic
- [@notesfromyourtherapist](https://www.instagram.com/notesfromyourtherapist/) — Allyson Dinneen, MFT, posts short handwritten notes on emotional regulation, boundaries, and anxiety
- [@annatheanxietycoach](https://www.instagram.com/annatheanxietycoach/) — Anna Papaioannou, integrative nervous system therapist focused on body-based approaches to anxiety and trauma

## Learn

### Books & Self-Help Guides

- [Hope and Help for Your Nerves](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/325366/hope-and-help-for-your-nerves-by-claire-weekes/) — Dr. Claire Weekes's classic guide on facing, accepting, and floating through anxiety symptoms rather than fighting them
- [Dare: The New Way to End Anxiety and Stop Panic Attacks Fast](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/26452130-dare) — Barry McDonagh's step-by-step technique for approaching panic instead of resisting it
- [The Anxiety and Phobia Workbook](https://www.newharbinger.com/9781648485572/the-anxiety-and-phobia-workbook/) — Edmund J. Bourne's evidence-based workbook, a 30-year staple covering panic, GAD, OCD, and phobias
- [Unwinding Anxiety](https://drjud.com/book/) — Judson Brewer's neuroscience-based approach to breaking habitual worry and fear loops
- [The Anxiety Healer's Guide](https://www.alisonseponara.com/book) — Alison Seponara's coping strategies and mindfulness techniques to calm the mind and body
- [Notes from Your Therapist](https://www.panmacmillan.com/authors/allyson-dinneen/notes-from-your-therapist/9781529073966) — Allyson Dinneen's illustrated, bite-sized insights on emotional regulation and boundaries

### Free Self-Help Workbooks & Courses

- [Centre for Clinical Interventions — Anxiety Self-Help Resources](https://www.cci.health.wa.gov.au/Resources/Looking-After-Yourself/Anxiety) — Free, clinically written workbooks and worksheets from a Western Australian public mental health service
- [Get Self Help](https://www.getselfhelp.co.uk/anxiety-self-help/) — Carol Vivyan's free CBT worksheets, information sheets, and self-help courses for anxiety, panic, and worry
- [HelpGuide — Anxiety](https://www.helpguide.org/mental-health/anxiety) — Nonprofit guide with practical articles on symptoms, self-help strategies, and when to seek treatment
- [MindShift CBT — Getting Started Guide](https://www.anxietycanada.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/Getting-Started-with-MindShift-CBT.pdf) — Anxiety Canada's free guided walkthrough of CBT strategies for worry, panic, and avoidance

## Apps & Tools

### CBT & Structured Programs

- [MindShift CBT](https://www.anxietycanada.com/resources/mindshift-cbt/) — Free app from the nonprofit Anxiety Canada using evidence-based CBT strategies to reduce worry, stress, and panic
- [Wysa](https://www.wysa.com/) — AI chatbot trained in CBT and DBT techniques for anonymous, 24/7 anxiety and stress support
- [Sanvello](https://www.sanvello.com/) — CBT-based mood and anxiety tracking app with an FDA Breakthrough Device Designation, accepted by some insurance plans
- [Unwinding Anxiety](https://drjud.com/) — Judson Brewer's award-winning app built on his mindfulness-based research into habitual worry

### Breathing, Grounding & Meditation

- [Rootd](https://www.rootd.io/) — Panic-button app with grounding exercises and breathing techniques for acute panic attacks
- [Breathwrk](https://www.breathwrk.com/) — Guided breathing exercises for stress and anxiety, with over 50 techniques paired with music and haptics
- [Calm](https://www.calm.com/) — Meditation and sleep app with an SOS feature for acute anxiety moments and calming Sleep Stories
- [Headspace](https://www.headspace.com/) — Meditation app whose structured Managing Anxiety course teaches observing anxious thoughts without engaging them

## Community & Support

### Forums & Online Communities

- [r/Anxiety](https://www.reddit.com/r/Anxiety/) — Reddit's largest anxiety-support community, with an extensive FAQ and hundreds of thousands of members
- [r/socialanxiety](https://www.reddit.com/r/socialanxiety/) — Peer support community focused specifically on social anxiety, coping strategies, and recovery stories

### Find a Therapist

- [Psychology Today — Find a Therapist](https://www.psychologytoday.com/us/therapists) — Searchable directory of licensed therapists filterable by specialty, insurance, and location
- [Open Path Collective](https://openpathcollective.org/) — Nonprofit connecting clients with therapists offering sliding-scale sessions from $30–80, after a one-time $65 membership fee

## Official Organizations & Research

- [Anxiety and Depression Association of America (ADAA)](https://adaa.org/) — International nonprofit dedicated to the prevention, treatment, and cure of anxiety, depression, OCD, and PTSD
- [Anxiety Canada](https://www.anxietycanada.com/) — Canadian charity behind the free MindShift CBT app and evidence-based anxiety resources
- [Anxiety UK](https://www.anxietyuk.org.uk/) — UK's largest user-led charity for anxiety disorders, running a helpline, text service, and therapy access
- [National Institute of Mental Health — Anxiety Disorders](https://www.nimh.nih.gov/health/topics/anxiety-disorders) — US government research agency's overview of anxiety disorder types, causes, and treatment
- [Mental Health America — Anxiety Screening](https://screening.mhanational.org/screening-tools/anxiety/) — Free, anonymous, scientifically validated GAD-7 anxiety screening tool
- [NAMI — National Alliance on Mental Illness](https://www.nami.org/) — Largest US grassroots mental health organization, offering a helpline and local support programs
- [Center for Anxiety & Related Disorders, Boston University](https://www.bu.edu/card/) — David H. Barlow's research center studying and treating anxiety and emotional disorders

## Crisis & Immediate Support

- [988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline](https://988lifeline.org/) — Free, confidential US crisis line — call or text 988, available 24/7 in English and Spanish plus 240+ languages via interpretation
- [Crisis Text Line](https://www.crisistextline.org/) — Free, 24/7 text-based crisis support — text HOME to 741741 in the US
- [Samaritans](https://www.samaritans.org/) — Free, 24/7 UK and Ireland emotional support line — call 116 123, no need to be in crisis to reach out
